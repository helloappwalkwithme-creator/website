What real days look like

This is not sample text. These are the exact messages you get.

Day 1

Selling is Serving

Discard the old image of the pushy salesperson. True sales means solving profound problems and guiding people from their current state to a better one.

Task: Before your first call today, write down 3 ways your offer (e.g., your coaching program) measurably improves your prospect's life.

Day 4

Controlling the Frame

In every meeting, two realities (frames) collide. The stronger reality absorbs the weaker. Don't be a beggar.

Task: At the start of your next call, define a clear time frame: "We have exactly 30 minutes to see if there is a fit. Then we decide the next step."

Day 30

The Micro-Close

Don't just focus on the final deal. Every step in the process requires a small "Yes" to maintain momentum.

Task: Never end a call today without sending and confirming a calendar invite for the next touchpoint.
