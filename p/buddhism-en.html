Day 1

The Breath as an Anchor (Anapanasati)

Mindfulness of breathing is the foundation of Buddhist practice. The breath is always in the present; it brings the wandering mind back to the here and now.

Task: Take exactly 60 seconds 3 times today to focus solely on your breath. When thoughts arise, gently acknowledge them and return to the breath.

Day 4

Beginner's Mind (Shoshin)

In the beginner's mind there are many possibilities, but in the expert's there are few. Preconceptions blind us to the reality of the moment.

Task: Listen to someone today (partner, colleague) as if you have never met them before. Block out everything you think you know and just be curious.

Day 30

Nirvana in the Here and Now

Nirvana is not an otherworldly place after death. It is the complete extinguishing of greed, hatred, and delusion in this exact moment. You are already there, you just have to notice it.

Task: For 5 minutes today, let go of the urge to improve yourself, plan the day, or solve problems. Just sit and allow yourself to be completely "done".
