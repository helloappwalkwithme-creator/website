Day 4

Complete Ownership

Sharing a task doesn't help if one still has to do the reminding. Complete ownership means: Conception, Planning, and Execution (CPE), all in one hand.

Your step todayTransfer exactly ONE recurring task (e.g., recycling, making breakfast) completely to your partner today. From planning to finish. Never interfere again.

Day 30

The New Relationship Contract

After 30 days, blind spots have cleared up. A fair relationship does not rely on "common sense", but on explicitly negotiated, visible agreements.

Your step todayWrite a formal, short "contract" today. Who is from today on the absolute manager (planner & executor) for which major life area in your relationship? Hang it up visibly.
