Day 1

The Renewal of the Mind

Romans 12:2: "Don't be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may prove what is the good, well-pleasing, and perfect will of God." (WEB) Real change starts on the inside, with what you let shape your thinking. Task: Identify one negative core thought today. Actively reframe it into a positive, empowering truth and write it down.

Day 4

The Principle of Rest

Genesis 2:2-3: "On the seventh day God finished his work which he had done; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had done. God blessed the seventh day, and made it holy, because he rested in it from all his work of creation which he had done." (WEB) Rest is God's idea, not a reward you have to earn. Task: Schedule a "sacred hour" today where you do absolutely nothing productive. No phone, no tasks, just being.

Day 30

Love as the Foundation

1 Corinthians 13:4-5: "Love is patient and is kind; love doesn't envy. Love doesn't brag, is not proud, doesn't behave itself inappropriately, doesn't seek its own way, is not provoked, takes no account of evil;" (WEB) Love is something you do, not only something you feel. Task: In a difficult situation today, act according to the principle: "What would love do now?" Choose connection.
